# Code of Conduct

SkinOS welcomes people with different backgrounds, experience levels and opinions. This policy applies to project issues, discussions, pull requests and other spaces maintained by the project.

Be respectful and specific. Critique code and ideas rather than people, accept reasonable feedback, credit others' work and allow contributors time to respond. Disagreement and blunt technical feedback are fine; harassment, threats, discrimination, sexual harassment, impersonation and publishing another person's private information are not.

Do not pressure people to share account credentials, private messages or personal information. Do not use project spaces to organize abuse of other users or services.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, ask a participant to change their behavior, restrict participation or remove access. Responses should be proportionate and take context and repeated behavior into account. Maintainers are also bound by this policy.

Use GitHub's report controls for abusive content. For a confidential project-specific concern, ask a maintainer for a private contact channel without posting sensitive details publicly. A moderation decision can be appealed to another maintainer if one is available; do not repost removed private information while appealing.
